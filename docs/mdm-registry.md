## Compare a task, not just a brand

For each candidate record: exact ownership/enrollment support; per-OS commands; app catalog and approval flow; policy timing and offline behavior; recovery and Activation Lock handling; identity; audit/RBAC; API/read-only access; privacy and data location; migration disruption; licensing and support; cost in the customer's region; and pilot results.

Replacing an MDM does not remove the need for a reachable device-management channel. Do not recommend migration to solve an offline command-delivery problem without evidence of a product-specific defect and a validated alternative.
