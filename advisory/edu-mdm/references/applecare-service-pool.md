# AppleCare for Enterprise — general service-pool study

Reviewed 2026-11-05. Source: [Apple ACE Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/applecare/applecareforenterprise/), §§2, 3.1, 3.5, 4.1. Current public terms inform general guidance; the purchased plan confirmation and applicable terms must be matched before a tenant-specific entitlement decision.

## Source-backed rules

Fault/battery service is distinct from pool fulfillment. Published iPad/iPhone limits are 5% or 10%; Mac/display limits are 2% or 4%, depending on the purchased option. Limits concern fulfillment requests over the plan term; no annual reset is stated. Replacement coverage follows the remaining term. Lost/stolen equipment is excluded. Failing the applicable return requirement, normally 14 business days, can incur retail charges. Further draws depend on remaining pool availability.

Inference: pool exhaustion alone does not cancel distinct fault-service benefits; Apple still assesses eligibility. No automatic refill, paid top-up right or extra accidental-damage entitlement was established by this review. Obtain written commercial confirmation instead of promising them.

## Evidence checklist and answer method

Request only the minimum sanitized facts needed: plan option and confirmation wording, covered product category and aggregate eligible-device basis, term dates, ledger date, used/remaining/adjustment entries, requested service category, and the exact written vendor proposal if there is one. Do not request agreement numbers, support PINs, device serials, full contact rosters or raw portal exports for a general explanation.

Do not turn a marketing maximum into a guaranteed purchased allowance. Label calculations illustrative until contract basis, rounding, adjustments and ledger semantics are established. Compare request history with the balance instead of equating the number of distinct damaged devices with the number of consumed requests. Do not infer a precise SPC/WUR expansion or credit conversion from a column heading.

If the pool is empty, classify the requested service before proposing a next step. Offer an Apple/AASP eligibility assessment or a written quote as appropriate, without asserting a price, refill availability, service guarantee or approval. A reseller's oral statement is evidence of what was said, not an established Apple contract change. Keep optional reseller coverage separate from Apple coverage. Do not contact anyone, submit a case, accept an agreement or purchase service unless separately instructed.

Parent charging, cost recovery and insurance procurement are separate school decisions, not consequences automatically dictated by this source. Do not invent a parent's financial liability or decide policy from a pool balance.

Use a result table: issue → evidence → confirmed rule / inference / unknown → minimal next verification. State what can be answered generally now and precisely what needs the agreement. A contract-specific unknown is a valid boundary, not permission to speculate.
