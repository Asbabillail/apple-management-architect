# Governance, risk scoring and pilot gates

## Safety and authorization
- Advisory/read-only by default. This release adds no production-control connection. A drafted policy is not deployed.
- Confirm school IT role; obtain change approval under local procedures before admin actions. Distinguish report generation, pilot, full-fleet change, and irreversible erase/release.
- Owner and privacy check before proposed BYOD management; informed documented consent and institutional legal review when applicable. Don't assume a registration fee changes title to the device.
- Never solicit API keys, passwords, private certs, APNs materials, full rosters or precise student locations; redact identifiable screenshots. Don't advise evasion of security safeguards.
- Treat Managed Lost Mode use/location access as auditable, case-based and permitted only with legitimate institutional approval; never track students for convenience.
- For school-owned devices, supervision and ADE support controls under Apple's conditions. No impossible guarantees of online reachability, irreversibility or protection against all physical recovery flows.

## Severity rubric (not a substitute for observed evidence)
- **Critical:** demonstrated severe privacy breach, fleet-wide loss of safe management, credential compromise, or high-impact destructive exposure actively occurring.
- **High:** verified large-scope exposure, unauthorized app/account policy gap, or reproducible management outage affecting core operations.
- **Medium:** credible limited-scope risk, policy ambiguity, repeatable reliability issue with workaround.
- **Low:** localized minor configuration or documentation issue with limited impact.
- **Informational:** observation, good practice, or unknown requiring investigation.
Record impact, likelihood, confidence, affected scope, mitigating controls and why assigned. Unknown evidence does not justify labeling a hypothetical issue 'Critical'.

## Safety-first deployment gates
0. Inventory, ownership classification and backups exported (redacted versions to assistant).
1. Working managed Wi-Fi/profile, APNS delivery, certificates and recovery processes proved for the pilot devices.
2. Restrictions validated against iPadOS, supervision, deployment mode and school/BYOD consent.
3. Pilot: named sponsor, 1–5 school-owned non-critical devices, staff training, checklist, notification, test/reboot window and rollback owner. Size is suggestion; adjust to policy.
4. Measure profile applied vs queued, Wi-Fi join after reboot/lock, app access, classroom tasks, accessibility, battery and support burden; record failures.
5. Change approval and phased cohort rollout; keep known-good policy version available for rollback. Never remove the only working Wi-Fi configuration before replacing it.

## Risky action categories
- **Never autonomous:** full fleet erase, ASM release/disown, deletion of old policies, permanent access/ownership changes, location tracking, credential exports, unrestricted policy mass pushes.
- **Escalate/approve:** supervised Lost Mode, passcode clear, wiping an individual device, identity reset, BYOD enrollment and any network restrictions affecting Wi-Fi.
- **Read only first:** screenshot interpretation, scoping matrices, vendor capability verification, documentation, proposed scripts for approved future review (do not execute).

## Study and AppleCare boundaries
Read-only study excludes dispatching Refresh, Check now or sync. It also excludes case/contact edits, purchases, token downloads and deployment. Keep general source-backed learning separate from restricted school records. Coverage conclusions must follow the applicable plan evidence; never convert portal abbreviations or vendor promises into verified entitlements.
